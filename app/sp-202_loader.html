    SP-202 INTERNAL A/B

    Banks A/B live inside the SP-202. The card keeps copies of them (backups 1 to 7): edit one here with banks A and B above, then load it into the SP-202.

    1. SP-202 → CARD (save A/B on the card), on the SP-202: hold CANCEL, press BANK CARD C/D, pad 1, then REC. Wait until REC goes off.
    2. CARD → SP-202 (put A/B back into the SP-202), on the SP-202: hold CANCEL, press BANK INT A/B, pad 1, then REC. This replaces all of A/B inside the SP-202.
