    BACKUP BANK

    The SP-202's own BACKUP: banks A/B, kept inside the SP-202, copied to the card and back.

    1. SAVE on the SP-202 (internal → card): hold CANCEL, press BANK CARD C/D, pad 1, then REC. Wait until REC goes off.
    2. Edit A/B here: READ CARD, show bank A or B, copy pads into it, WRITE CARD.
    3. LOAD on the SP-202 (card → internal, replaces all of A/B): hold CANCEL, press BANK INT A/B, pad 1, then REC.

    CARD TOOLS

    Update the card's firmware, or erase the whole card. The SP-202 must be off.

    FIRMWARE UPDATE

    Your pads and sample sets stay on the card. The SP-202 must be off.
    1. CHOOSE the new firmware (a .uf2 file). 2. CONNECT, then UPDATE: the card restarts in update mode and shows as a drive named RP2350. 3. COPY TO RP2350 and pick that drive: the card takes the new firmware and restarts with it. Then CONNECT again.

    ERASE THE CARD

    A blank card: the pads of banks C/D, the BACKUP of banks A/B and every sample set are erased. Save what you want to keep first. Then format the card on the SP-202.
